What we can help with

  • Account and sign-in issues
  • Creating or joining a Family
  • Linking a Child device
  • Voice recording and playback
  • Notifications
  • Family Vault access
  • Account deletion

When contacting support, please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Please do not email your Recovery Key, verification codes, passwords, or private family voice recordings.
