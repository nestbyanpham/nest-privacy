Nest

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026
Last updated: September 5, 2026

Nest is a family communication and memory app. This Privacy Policy explains the information Nest processes, why it is needed, how it is protected, and the choices available to families.

1. Information Nest processes

Depending on how a family uses Nest, the service may process:

  • Parent account information: Parent email address, authentication identifiers, session information, and Parent profile information used to sign in and operate the account.
  • Family and child information: Family profile information, child profile information, Contributions, completion state, family conversation metadata, and related timestamps.
  • Voice content: Child Voice Capsules and Parent voice responses that a family chooses to record and store.
  • Device and notification information: App/device identifiers, platform information, optional device labels, push-notification tokens, notification delivery state, and related operational timestamps.
  • Security and abuse-prevention information: Security identifiers, rate-limit records, and hashed network-related values used to prevent misuse. Nest is designed not to persist raw IP addresses in its Join Code rate-limit records.
  • Permission-dependent information: Microphone access is used only when a person chooses to record voice. Notification permission is used only to deliver Nest notifications when enabled.

Nest does not use analytics SDKs or behavioral advertising to profile family activity. Production advertising is disabled in this release.

2. How information is used

Nest processes information only as needed to authenticate Parents and Child devices; operate a Family; create Contributions and family conversations; store and play family voice content; deliver notifications; recover or share Family Vault access; protect the service; and support account deletion, security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

Nest does not sell personal information.

3. Family Vault and encryption

Nest is designed so that sensitive family content is encrypted before it is uploaded where the Family Vault architecture applies. Family Vault keys are generated and handled on family devices, and encrypted key envelopes are stored by the backend so approved family devices can obtain access.

Certain service information must remain readable by infrastructure in order to operate Nest, such as Parent email used for authentication, account and device identifiers, notification tokens, timestamps, status values, and security metadata.

Data sent between the app and service providers is protected in transit using encrypted network connections supported by those providers.

4. Service providers

Nest relies on infrastructure providers to operate the app. Current technical integrations include:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage, realtime/backend functions, and related infrastructure.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for push-notification delivery.
  • Google Mobile Ads components are included in some app builds for a voluntary Parent-initiated support feature. Production advertising is disabled in this release.

Push notifications are designed to use an opaque notification reference rather than placing private family content in the push payload.

These providers process information on Nest's behalf according to their own contractual and security obligations. Platform providers such as Apple or Google may also process information required to distribute the app, deliver notifications, or provide operating-system services.

Nest does not use advertising to profile family activity or send private family voice content to advertising networks.

5. Children and family use

Nest is designed for families with children. A Parent creates and manages the Family. Child access is designed around a family-controlled device link rather than requiring a child to create a permanent email-based account.

Child-related information and voice content are used for the Family experience and are not intended for public profiles or public discovery.

Parents should only add children and family content when they have authority to do so.

6. Retention and deletion

Nest keeps information while it is needed to operate a Family, provide requested features, protect the service, or meet applicable legal and security obligations.

A Parent can choose Settings → Account → Delete my account.

  • If another Parent remains in a Family, the departing Parent's account, credentials, device access, notification access, and Family Vault access are removed. Shared Family memories remain with the surviving Family and are no longer linked to the deleted Parent where attribution can be detached.
  • If the deleting Parent is the last Parent in a Family, Nest permanently deletes that Family and its associated Family data, linked Child device identities, encrypted family content, stored voice content, memories, notifications, and Family Vault server data.
  • Local Nest account, Vault, notification, queued voice, and related private device state are cleared as part of the deletion flow.

Limited security or abuse-prevention records may be retained only when reasonably necessary to protect the service or meet legal obligations. Such records are not used to restore deleted Family access.

7. Privacy choices

Families can decide when to record and send voice content; control microphone permission through device settings; control notification permission through device settings and Nest notification controls; use Family Vault recovery controls where available; and delete a Parent account from Nest Settings.

If a Parent no longer has access to the app, privacy questions or deletion assistance can be submitted at tri.dered92@icloud.com.

8. International processing

Nest's infrastructure providers may process information in countries or regions where they operate. When Nest selects production infrastructure, those providers and regions should be reflected accurately in release disclosures and store privacy declarations.

9. Changes to this policy

Nest may update this Privacy Policy when the product, service providers, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of the policy will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact Nest at tri.dered92@icloud.com.
